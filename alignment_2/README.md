### Data: `mec-variation`

The notebooks in this folder use the `mec-variation` dataset (scores in MusicXML and MEI, performances in MIDI and audio, and alignments of a variation of Mozart's *12 Variations on "Ah, vous dirai-je, maman"* K. 265), which is in the folder `alignment_1/mec-variation`. The dataset comes from the repository of the workshop *Music Alignment Uncovered: Representations, Algorithms and Hands-On Tools*, held at the Music Encoding Conference 2026:

> Carlos Cancino-Chacón, Jiyun Park, Silvan Peter, Yigitcan Özer, and Suhit Chiruthapudi. *Music Alignment Uncovered: Representations, Algorithms and Hands-On Tools*. Music Encoding Conference 2026. <https://github.com/pymatchmaker/mec2026_alignment_workshop>
